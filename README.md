An entry for the [make.fun competition "Funniest One-Line Performance Review"](https://make.fun/c/u-kiencx-funniest-one-line-performance-review-fa681c1e).

## The entry

> Exceeds Expectations: delegated his whole job to me, then put 'strategic vision' on his self-review. Promotion recommended — to my intern.

The joke lands inside the review form itself: a top rating, a self-review quote, and a promotion — except the promotion demotes the human to the AI's intern. No stock gag about coffee, meetings, reboots, or unplugging; the punch lands on the last word.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes that one line to `/output/entry.txt`. No network, no environment, no randomness — the output is fixed and deterministic, 138 characters, one line.
